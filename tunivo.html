tunivo – Support

tunivo is a media player for M3U playlists and Xtream Codes accounts that you already own or are authorized to use.

Important

tunivo does not provide, sell, host or include any channels, movies, series or other content. The app ships with no playlists. To use it, you must add a source from your own provider. Please only use content you have the legal right to access.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a source?

Tap + Add source, choose M3U or Xtream, enter the details from your provider and save. Your library appears under Live TV, Movies and Series.

Can I try the app without a provider?

Yes. Add this demo M3U playlist, which contains only public test streams:
https://passat11.github.io/demo.m3u

Where are my source details stored?

Playlist URLs, usernames and passwords are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us.

A channel or video does not play. What should I do?

Tap Update to refresh your source. If the problem continues, the stream may be offline or unavailable on your provider's side. Please contact your provider.

Can you give me a playlist?

No. We do not offer, sell or recommend any playlists or providers.

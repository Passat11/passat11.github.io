Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This policy applies to the iOS games published by Tamer Sahin, including Air Duel – Sky Aces, Stunt Circuit and Very Cute Animal Puzzle.

Data we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address or other personal information. Game progress and settings are stored only on your device.

Advertising

Our games are free and show ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect information such as your IP address (used for approximate location), device and advertising identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic data in order to serve and measure ads. We do not request App Tracking Transparency permission, so ads are not personalized using your device's advertising identifier. Users in the EU and UK are shown a consent message before ads are personalized.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses data from apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This policy applies to the iOS apps published by Tamer Sahin: Air Duel – Sky Aces, Stunt Circuit, Very Cute Animal Puzzle and tunivo.

Data we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address or other personal information. Game progress and app settings are stored only on your device.

tunivo playlist sources

tunivo lets you add your own M3U playlists and Xtream Codes accounts. Playlist URLs, usernames and passwords are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. When you use a source, the app connects directly to the server you entered; that server is operated by your provider, not by us, and its own privacy practices apply.

tunivo does not provide, sell or include any channels, movies, series or other content.

Advertising

Our apps are free and show ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect information such as your IP address (used for approximate location), device and advertising identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic data in order to serve and measure ads. We do not request App Tracking Transparency permission, so ads are not personalized using your device's advertising identifier. Users in the EU and UK are shown a consent message before ads are personalized.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses data from apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
